Joystick-controlled RC servo

An analog joystick sets a servo angle through FPGA logic and a PWM output.

From joystick position to motion

In Quartus, we scaled the 12-bit ADC reading from 0–4095 to a target angle from 0–180°. The FPGA displayed the selected angle and generated a pulse-width command for the RC servo.

Checking the output

We used an ADALM2000 oscilloscope to check how the pulse changed with the command. We measured 0.606 ms at 0°, 1.611 ms at 100°, and about 2 ms at 180°. Comparing those readings connected the FPGA output to the motion we saw on the bench.
