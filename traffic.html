Intersection traffic controller

Our three-person team built a C++ simulator to study how an intersection handles everyday traffic and prioritizes an approaching emergency vehicle.

View source on GitHub ↗

Modeling an intersection

We represented north–south and east–west traffic with separate vehicle queues. During normal operation, the signal sequence determines which direction can move. When an emergency vehicle approaches, the controller changes the sequence to give its direction priority.

My contribution

I helped define how the controller interacts with the TrafficLight and Vehicle classes and contributed to both classes. I divided development tasks among teammates, reviewed how the components fit together, and tested and debugged the integrated simulator in Linux.
